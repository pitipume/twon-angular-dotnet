# Business Overview

## What is Twon?

Twon is a dual-commerce platform combining two products:

1. **Ebook Shop** — customers buy and read digital books in-browser (no download allowed)
2. **E-Tarot Card Shop** — customers buy tarot card decks and use them in-browser (shuffle, draw, spread)

## Background

- Built for a Thai family business
- Father runs a physical tarot card shop — ~2M THB revenue over 4 years
- Goal: bring the business online, serve Thailand + international customers
- Must be scalable from day one but start cheap (free/low-cost hosting)

## Goals

| Priority | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1 | Customers can buy ebooks and read them in browser |
| 2 | Customers can buy tarot decks and use them in browser |
| 3 | Admin can upload content (PDF ebooks, ZIP of card images) |
| 4 | Manual PromptPay payment (Thai bank QR + slip upload) |
| 5 | Admin approves payment → customer gets access |
| Future | Stripe/Omise payment gateway |
| Future | Premium tier (ebook download) |
| Future | Mobile app via Capacitor.js |

## Target market

- Primary: Thailand (Thai language content, PromptPay payment)
- Secondary: International (English content, card payments later)
- All ages — design must be accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA minimum)
